**Working title:** Global Signals  
**Status:** Experimental  
**Route:** `/side-trails/global-signals/`

---

## Tagline

Understanding how world events shape everyday life.

---

## Purpose

Global Signals helps people understand how geopolitics, trade, infrastructure,
weather, conflict, energy, cyber events, and government policy ripple through
supply chains, industries, and ultimately affect citizens.

---

## Mission

Make distant causes legible at home — sourced relationships from world events
to everyday consequence, without panic or partisan theater.

---

## Philosophy

| Pillar | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Relationships first | Events matter because of what they connect |
| Sources over spectacle | Citations, dates, confidence; empty stays empty |
| Citizen-scale clarity | Explain why a distant signal might matter nearby |
| Not a news feed | No engagement ranking, doom scroll, or invented alerts |
