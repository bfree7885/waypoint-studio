# SignalTerrain Vision

**Status:** Active foundation  
**Tagline:** Understand the world's signals.  
**Audience:** Product, editorial, design, and future RF / cyber / infrastructure / research workspaces  
**Product verb:** Monitor ([Product Framework](WAYPOINT-PRODUCT-FRAMEWORK.md))

SignalTerrain is Waypoint Studio’s home for **Signal Intelligence** — a calm platform for observing, understanding, and connecting signals from the modern world.

A **signal** is any piece of information that improves situational awareness: radio transmissions, advisories, vulnerabilities, campaigns, satellites, routing, space weather, government alerts, outages, research, standards, and carefully framed current events.

---

## What this is not

Do **not** treat SignalTerrain as:

- An advanced RF toolkit (SDR demodulators, packet capture suites)  
- A vulnerability scanner  
- An offensive security product  
- A SIEM  
- Another RSS / news reader  
- A fear or hype engine  

Those are permanent non-goals for this product identity. Future workspaces expand **this foundation**, not disconnected gadgets.

---

## Mission

Help people **observe, understand, and connect** signals so they leave knowing more than when they arrived.

| Value | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Evidence | Claims rest on sources |
| Context | What surrounds the fact |
| Historical perspective | How the topic evolved |
| Connections | How topics relate |
| Transparency | Confidence, unknowns, limits |
| Curiosity | Invite deeper reading |
| Calm analysis | No alarm theater |
| Respect for uncertainty | Silence and “unknown” are honest |

Not fear. Not hype. Not speed-for-its-own-sake.

---

## Philosophy

SignalTerrain should feel like an experienced park ranger for invisible environments: notice what matters, explain why, show evidence, then let the user decide.

## Workspaces (future homes on one foundation)

| Workspace | Intent |
|-----------|--------|
| **RF** | Receivers, frequencies, propagation, logging, audio, transcripts, signal catalog |
| **Cyber** | Vulnerabilities, advisories, campaigns, defensive knowledge — literacy, not offense |
| **Infrastructure** | Routing, cloud, comms, power, cellular, GPS, disruptions |
| **Research** | Papers, RFCs, standards, government pubs, Waypoint knowledge |

Workspaces share **topics**, **relationships**, and **platform systems** — they are not separate products.

---

## Topic-first identity

Everything important becomes a **topic** that evolves over time: a CVE, a frequency, a satellite, a protocol, an agency, a paper, a provider.

Topics are not stale cards. They accumulate timeline, sources, analysis, and relationships.

See [Topic Model](SIGNALTERRAIN-TOPIC-MODEL.md) and [Relationship Model](SIGNALTERRAIN-RELATIONSHIP-MODEL.md).

---

## Experience tone

Professional. Modern. Quiet. Focused. Thoughtful. Readable. Fast.

No unnecessary visual noise.  
No engagement tricks.  
No sensationalism.
