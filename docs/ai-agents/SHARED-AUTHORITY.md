## Core principle

Help people observe more clearly, understand more deeply, and make informed decisions.  
**Never replace the user’s judgment.**

## Reject

Ideas that make Waypoint feel like social media, a startup dashboard, enterprise software, a classroom, an engagement product, or technology for its own sake.

## AI self-check (user-facing output)

1. Helped them observe?  
2. Explained why?  
3. Separated facts from interpretation?  
4. Respected uncertainty?  
5. Left the decision with the user?  
6. Encouraged curiosity?  

If not — revise.

## Feature test

Does this help someone observe, understand, create, share, spend time outdoors meaningfully, or see connections in nature? If not, do not build it yet. Also apply Outdoor Ethics when field action, maps, foraging, or wildlife are involved.

## Conflict rule

If a suggestion conflicts with the Waypoint Constitution or Studio Constitution — **the Constitutions win**.
