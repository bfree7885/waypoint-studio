# SignalTerrain V0.5

**Status:** In development · unpublished · `noindex`  
**App route:** `/apps/summit-signal/` (do not occupy `/apps/signalterrain/`)  
**This release:** Prove the V0.4 Activation Zone engine on a second real W2/GC summit

> **SignalTerrain (SOTA/outdoor, unpublished) is a new product definition and is not the retired SignalTerrain Cyber product.**

> **SignalTerrain remains unpublished.** It is not in public navigation, the homepage, About/Support, or the sitemap. `noindex` and `robots.txt` Disallow stay in force.

> **SignalTerrain's Activation Zone is a terrain-derived planning aid. Operators remain responsible for complying with current SOTA rules and verifying their activation.**

> **Geographic presence inside the mapped zone is not a valid SOTA activation.**

V0.4 remains the Activation Zone engine, 25 m closed-contour rule, and Slide Mountain acceptance path. V0.5 expands terrain intelligence: the same general algorithm, a second labeled USGS 3DEP fixture, no product redesign.

---

## Goal

Show that the V0.4 Activation Zone implementation works for more than one real W2/GC summit using deterministic 3DEP-derived fixture data. No summit-specific algorithm hacks. The Activation Zone is not a radius. No live SOTA, 3DEP, Valhalla, or Overpass in CI.

---

## Second summit: Hunter Mountain `W2/GC-002`

**Why this summit**

- Already present in the loaded W2/GC catalogue (`data/ss-summits-w2-gc.json`) and used as the canonical second summit in V0.1 nearby / V0.2 tests.
- Valid SOTA record: Greater Catskills, 10 points, Maidenhead `FN22ve`.
- Reliable USGS 3DEP 1 m lidar coverage in the Catskills.
- Terrain is a high plateau with a southeast spine, not a trivial cone copy of Slide. That exercises 4-connected flood fill, cell-edge outlining, and window sizing on elongated qualifying ground.
- Distinct massif from Slide (Hunter is ~25 km northeast).

Not chosen: Black Dome `W2/GC-003` (kept as the honest no-fixture W2 control).

## Provider

- `W2/GC-001` → Slide fixture
- `W2/GC-002` → Hunter fixture
- Other loaded W2 summits without a fixture → `unsupported-region` unless `?az=live=1` / `?route=live=1` (live 3DEP)
- Non-W2 without a fixture → `unsupported-region` unless live
- Fixture vs live results are cached separately so requesting live DEM is not blocked by a prior unsupported fixture miss
- Never a circular/radius Activation Zone

## Limitations

- Only two W2/GC summits have labeled AZ fixtures. Other catalogue summits stay honestly unavailable unless live DEM is requested.
- Hunter has no access/route/elevation-profile fixtures in V0.5. Route-vs-AZ entry distance is still Slide-only in this milestone. V0.6 adds labeled Hunter hike fixtures.
- Analysis grid is 10 m; native 3DEP is 1 m. The polygon is stair-stepped at cell edges.
- Hunter’s SOTA elevation (1234 m) is 6.6 m above 3DEP at the pin (grid max 1229.4 m). Threshold uses SOTA.
- 4-connected flood fill; a diagonal-only ridge may split.
- FOSSGIS Valhalla remains development-only. SOTA API/data license is unresolved. `ARM-W2.pdf` was not independently retrieved.
- SignalTerrain is **unpublished**.
