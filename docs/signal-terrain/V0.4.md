> **SignalTerrain (SOTA/outdoor, unpublished) is a new product definition and is not the retired SignalTerrain Cyber product.**

> **SignalTerrain's Activation Zone is a terrain-derived planning aid. Operators remain responsible for complying with current SOTA rules and verifying their activation.**

## Authoritative SOTA rule

**Source:** Summits on the Air General Rules, English version **1.21**, authorised **1 June 2022**.  
Linked from [sota.org.uk/Joining-In/General-Rules](https://www.sota.org.uk/Joining-In/General-Rules)  
PDF: `https://storage.sota.org.uk/docs/SOTA-General-Rules-June-2022.pdf`  
Document reference **S0.1**. Reviewed **2026-09-04**. Not scraped from SOTA Goat.

**Activation Zone definition (GR §3.5 and §3.7.1(4)):**

Each Association defines a **Vertical Distance** from the precise summit. That distance defines a **contour outlining an Activation Zone**. The Vertical Distance will **normally be 25 metres**. For other than 25 m the Association Manager must provide a justifiable case; the Management Team may review it.

The operating position **must lie within a closed contour line** at that Vertical Distance below the summit. The operating position is the position of the **operator**. Typically the contour is 25 metres below peak height.

This is **not** a horizontal radius and **not** a buffer around the summit coordinate.

**W2 / Greater Catskills:** The SOTA API lists `ARM-W2.pdf` v1.3 (2010-05-01). That PDF was not independently retrievable from the public ARM URLs at review time. General Rules take precedence. No AM exception for other than 25 m is on file. SignalTerrain therefore applies the **25 m default** to W2/GC-001, with the association override table left empty for future ARM exceptions.

**W1 note (does not apply to W2):** the W1 ARM adds a separate non-motorised 100 ft / 33 m ascent interpretation. That is **not** copied onto W2.

**Other expedition rules (shown as text, not scored):** not in/near a motor vehicle; carry equipment; portable power; at least four QSOs for points. SignalTerrain V0.4 does not evaluate those.

## Summit elevation authority

The Activation **threshold uses the SOTA catalogue elevation** (`altM` / `elevationM`).

USGS 3DEP at the summit coordinate is a **quality check**, not a silent replacement of the SOTA record.

Slide Mountain `W2/GC-001`: SOTA **1277 m**. 3DEP at the coordinate **~1274.9 m** (grid max **1275.4 m**). Difference ~1.6–2.1 m. Surfaced in the AZ panel. Threshold remains **1277 − 25 = 1252 m**.

Material discrepancy (≥ 5 m) is labeled.

**Ordering (`cb627c08`):** if the DEM cell nearest the SOTA coordinate is below the Activation threshold, the engine first tries the highest qualifying neighbour in a 5×5 window. It emits `elevation-conflict` (or `calculation-failed`) only after that neighbour search finds nothing. Neighbour snap happens **before** elevation-conflict.

## Algorithm

1. Threshold = SOTA summit elevation − association Vertical Distance (25 m for W2).
2. Mark DEM cells with elevation ≥ threshold.
3. Find the DEM cell nearest the SOTA coordinate. If that cell is below threshold, **snap to the highest qualifying neighbour in a 5×5** (`cb627c08`). Only if no neighbour qualifies does the engine emit `elevation-conflict` / `calculation-failed`. Then **4-connected flood fill** from the seed.
4. Discard above-threshold cells that are **not** connected to this summit (neighbouring peak / high ground).
5. Trace a **closed polygon** around the connected mask by walking shared cell edges (square footprints of qualifying DEM cells). This is not marching-squares interpolation and not a radius.
6. Largest ring is the Activation Zone polygon.

Not a convex hull. Not a circle. Not every cell in the window above the threshold.

If qualifying cells touch the DEM window edge, the result is `ok` with `edgeClipped` and an honest note. Slide’s zone is interior to the 500 m window (568 cells, no edge).

## Route relationship

The V0.3 Valhalla geometry is **not rewritten**.

Walk the route. Test point-in-polygon. Record the first outside→inside crossing (binary search on the segment). Distance to entry is along-route haversine from the start. Remaining distance is the rest of the same geometry toward the summit coordinate.

States: enters / does not enter / route unavailable / AZ unavailable.

## Geolocation

If GPS is granted **and** AZ is `ok`: **Inside mapped Activation Zone** or **Outside mapped Activation Zone**.

Otherwise: **Location unavailable** or **AZ unavailable**.

Never substitutes straight-line distance to the summit. Never says “activated.”

## Polygon generation

The polygon is the outer **cell-edge outline** of the 4-connected qualifying mask. Each DEM cell is treated as a square footprint. Shared edges cancel; the remaining boundary is stitched into rings. The largest ring is the Activation Zone.

This is **not** a radius, **not** a buffer around the SOTA coordinate, and **not** interpolated marching squares (thin ridges and single-cell peaks failed that method).

## Activation Readiness

A factual known/unknown summary. Not a score. Groups: Summit, Access, Hike, Activation zone, Not in V0.4 (radio / weather).

Unknowns stay explicit (`Unavailable`, `Location unavailable`, `AZ unavailable`, `Not integrated`).

## Accuracy limitations

- Horizontal: analysis grid is **10 m**; native 3DEP at Slide is 1 m lidar. The polygon follows 10 m cell edges, so the contour is stair-stepped at that scale.
- Vertical: 3DEP bilinear samples; NAVD88-style metres. SOTA catalogue elevation can differ (Slide: 2.1 m). Threshold uses the SOTA value.
- Connectivity is **4-connected**. A diagonal-only ridge may split.
- Window: 500 m box around the summit. If qualifying terrain hits the window edge, the polygon may be truncated (`edgeClipped`).
- Route/AZ intersection uses the Valhalla polyline vs the polygon, not a field-verified contour.
- GPS inside/outside is geographic only.
