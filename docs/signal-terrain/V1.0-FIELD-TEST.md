# SignalTerrain first field test

**Unlisted URL (not private):**  
https://bfree7885.github.io/waypoint-studio-site/apps/summit-signal/

**Build:** V0.9 · `4f0c083`  
**Primary rehearsal:** Slide Mountain `W2/GC-001`  
**Regression only:** Hunter Mountain `W2/GC-002`

SignalTerrain is **planning software**. It is not emergency navigation, not safety equipment, not guaranteed offline, and not a legal-access authority. Carry the map/GPS backup you would take without this app. Being inside the Activation Zone is **not** a valid SOTA activation.

Do not add `?live=1`, `?access=live=1`, `?route=live=1`, or `?az=live=1`.

---

## Before leaving home

- [ ] Open the URL on the phone over HTTPS. Leave this tab open for the rest of the outing.
- [ ] Search **Slide** → **Slide Mountain W2/GC-001**.
- [ ] Confirm identity: Slide Mountain · W2/GC-001 · 1277 m / 4190 ft.
- [ ] In Access, tap the **name** of **Slide Mountain Parking Area** (not Giant Ledge). Inspect before hiking.
- [ ] Confirm start: `42.00868, -74.42764` · mapped parking · OSM `way/816358667`.
- [ ] **Copy coordinates**. **Open in Maps** (this hands driving to Apple/Google Maps — SignalTerrain does not route on roads).
- [ ] **Start hike here**. Review route ~3.6 mi / 5.8 km, gain ~1,753 ft / 534 m, time ~2 hr.
- [ ] Review Activation Zone: terrain contour, not a radius; threshold 4,108 ft / 1,252 m. Route enters AZ at ~3.4 mi / 5.6 km.
- [ ] Open Activation Plan + Verify before you go. Tick personal checklist items you actually use.
- [ ] Hide the sheet (**Close**), look at the map, **Show plan** — plan should still be there.
- [ ] Phone battery + backup navigation packed.
- [ ] Keep the SignalTerrain tab loaded. Closing or reloading it **without network** loses the app.

## At the parking area

- [ ] Does the real lot match Slide Mountain Parking Area / those coordinates?
- [ ] Note anything SignalTerrain called “mapped” that is wrong, closed, or unsigned.
- [ ] Reopen SignalTerrain (same tab). Is the plan still there?
- [ ] If you must pick a different mapped start: inspect it, then **Start hike here** (inspect alone does not change the route).
