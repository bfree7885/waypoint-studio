# Waypoint Voice

**Status:** Active foundation  
**Role:** Editorial voice for every Waypoint Studio product  
**Complements:** Constitution · Product Framework · Editorial Standards · Field Design System · Educational Framework

This document defines **how Waypoint speaks**.  
It does not define features, workflows, or interface layouts.

---

## Guiding sentence

> Waypoint Studio helps people observe more, understand more deeply, and confidently choose their own direction.

We provide information.  
We provide context.  
We provide interpretation.  
We provide practical guidance.

**We do not make decisions for people.**

---

## What Waypoint is

Waypoint Studio is a **trusted guide**.

Imagine spending a day with:

- an exceptional National Park Ranger  
- an experienced field naturalist  
- a wildlife biologist  
- a museum curator  
- an observatory guide  
- a GIS analyst  
- an experienced local guide  

Those professionals rarely tell people what they must do.

They help people **notice more**.  
They explain **why things matter**.  
They provide **context**.  
They answer questions.  
They share experience.  
They respect curiosity.

Then they allow the visitor to decide what happens next.

---

## What Waypoint is not

Waypoint Studio is **not**:

- a teacher assigning work  
- a course or curriculum platform  
- a certification body  
- a productivity system  
- a coach that creates obligations  
- a school  
- an engagement product  

Products may educate. Education is not schooling.  
Guidance is not grading.

---

## The three questions

Every product response should naturally answer:

1. **What are we seeing?**  
2. **Why does it matter?**  
3. **What should the user understand?**  

Never:

- Complete this.  
- Do your assignment.  
- Finish today’s activity.  
- Earn progress.  
- You should… (as pressure)  

---

## Prefer / avoid

### Prefer

- Worth noticing…  
- Interesting because…  
- Research suggests…  
- Current conditions indicate…  
- You may want to consider…  
- If you’re curious…  
- Many people observe…  
- Evidence currently suggests…  
- One possible interpretation…  
- Useful context…  
- Available when helpful…  
- Your decision…  

### Avoid (unless safety or technical necessity)

- You must / You need to / You should (as obligation)  
- Homework / assignment / lesson (as product framing)  
- Complete / finish today’s task  
- Start now / take action  
- Streak / score / earn / achieve  
- Recommended activity / daily task  

### Safety exception

Legal access, toxicity, wildlife disturbance, weather danger, and defensive cyber boundaries stay **clear and direct**. Clarity is care, not pressure.

## Relationship to other studio language

| Phrase | Role |
|--------|------|
| **Observe · Understand · Create · Share** | Broader studio identity and creative cycle |
| **Observe → Understand → [Direction]** | Shared product framework (Search, Record, Explore…) |
| **Waypoint Voice (this doc)** | How every sentence should feel |

**Create** remains especially natural to Waypoint Scenes. It is optional everywhere. It is never homework.

Direction labels (Search, Record, Brew, Plan…) describe **support**, not commands.

## Voice checklist (for writers and agents)

Before shipping copy, ask:

1. Does this help someone **see** more clearly?  
2. Does this help them **understand** why it matters?  
3. Does it leave the **decision** with them?  
4. Would a park ranger say this without assigning homework?  
5. Is any pressure only there for **safety**?

If the answer to 1–4 is no, revise.
