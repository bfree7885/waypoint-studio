## Positioning (locked for foundation)

- **Title:** Global Signals  
- **Tagline:** Understanding how world events shape everyday life.  
- **Not** a news website. **Not** financial advice.  
- **Is** a relationship intelligence platform.

## Explicit non-goals

- Engagement farming, doom scroll, or urgency hacks  
- Partisan framing or invented “breaking” events  
- Financial advice or trading tips  
- Offensive cyber tooling  
- Fabricating graph edges or citizen profiles  

---

## Review gate

Ship dates are intentionally absent. Module work requires a separate owner-approved
implementation branch after the foundation story is accepted.
