# SignalTerrain — Editorial Standards

**Status:** Active foundation  
**Complements:** [EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md](EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) (studio general) · [WAYPOINT-EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md](WAYPOINT-EDITORIAL-STANDARDS.md) (curated knowledge depth)  
**Vision:** [SIGNALTERRAIN-VISION.md](SIGNALTERRAIN-VISION.md)

These standards govern SignalTerrain topics, Signal Cards, workspace copy, and sample intelligence material. They **extend** studio editorial rules; they do not replace them.

---

## Core stance

Evidence over speculation.  
Context over headlines.  
Transparency over mystery.  
Calm over fear.

Users should leave **understanding more** — not more anxious.

---

## Required layers on important topics

| Layer | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Source Summary / Overview | What sources say — not what we wish they said |
| Waypoint Analysis (Perspective) | Clearly labeled editorial judgment |
| Known facts | Narrow, supportable statements |
| Unknowns | Always present; never hide gaps |
| Confidence | Shared Trust / SI labels — no decorative % |
| Limitations | Scope and freshness |
| Primary sources | Prefer originals |

Never merge Perspective into the source voice.

---

## Cyber and threat literacy

Allowed: cited advisories, vulnerability awareness, campaign literacy, defensive hygiene reminders.

Forbidden in product content:

- Exploit code, PoCs, weaponized payloads  
- Attack tutorials or credential testing guidance  
- Presenting rumor as attributed fact  
- “You are compromised” certainty without evidence  
- Sensational threat scores or countdown theater  

Threat actor and malware topics are **educational context**, not entertainment dossiers.

---

## RF and listening

- Encourage lawful practice; attach legality notes where relevant.  
- Do not invent spectrum activity.  
- Public-safety topics require compliance reminders.  
- Private audio and precise receiver locations stay local-first.

---

## Infrastructure and outages

- Prefer primary status pages and official bulletins.  
- Label single-source and unverified reports.  
- Stable / quiet states are editorial features — say what is *not* disrupted when honesty allows.

---

## Research and standards

- Cite DOI, RFC, or canonical URLs when available.  
- Distinguish preprint, draft, and adopted standard.  
- Historical documents stay labeled historical.

---

## Samples and demos

Foundation samples must use `meta.status: sample` (or equivalent) and disclaimers.  
Never present sample CVEs, actors, or advisories as live intelligence.

---

## Corrections

If a topic is wrong: correct timeline, note the change, keep provenance. Prefer evolve-in-place over silent rewrite.

---

## Voice

Park-ranger calm. Short sentences. Specific nouns. No engagement bait. No school-like pressure language.

Align with [WAYPOINT-VOICE.md](WAYPOINT-VOICE.md) and [Guide Experience](WAYPOINT-GUIDE-EXPERIENCE.md).
