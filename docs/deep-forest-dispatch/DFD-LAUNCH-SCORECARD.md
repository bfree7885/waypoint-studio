## Interpretation rules

What a pattern usually means (directionally — not automatic action):

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Low impressions** | Distribution, topic, or packaging may be weak — **or it is simply too early**. Do not over-correct on Day 3–7 alone. |
| **Impressions + low CTR** | Title / thumbnail (or search snippet) packaging problem is the first place to look. |
| **Good CTR + poor retention** | Opening, pacing, or story execution problem after the click. |
| **Good retention + low impressions** | Content may be solid; YouTube has not distributed it much yet. Prefer patience + packaging tests over rewriting the whole concept. |
| **Search impressions + low clicks** | Possible title / snippet / intent mismatch on the SERP. |
| **No search impressions yet** | **Do not conclude articles failed.** Indexing and ranking often need weeks. |

**Do not invent pass/fail quotas** (e.g. “X views or fail”). Early zero/near-zero is common for a new channel and a new library.

## Video #2 release plan (do not publish in this task)

**Recommended window:** about **3–5 days after Video #1** went public (existing plan).  
Video #2 assets are ready; **do not publish until the owner chooses that window.**

### Inspect from Video #1 before releasing #2

- Impressions vs CTR (is packaging getting clicks when shown?)
- Avg % viewed / early retention drop (first 10–30 seconds)
- Top traffic sources (Browse / Suggested / Search / External)
- Whether Mount Hood companion got any Waypoint traffic or embed plays

### Observations that may alter Video #2 *packaging* (title/thumb/description CTA only)

- Clear low CTR with non-trivial impressions → tighten title/thumb contrast before upload
- Strong early drop-off tied to a confusing cold open pattern → consider a clearer first 5–10s for #2 (packaging/open only — not a remake)
- Description CTA: keep companion URL pattern that matches Video #1

### Early numbers that should **not** delay Video #2

- Near-zero impressions in the first 1–3 days
- No Search Console clicks yet
- No subscribers yet
- Mount Hood article still near-zero views

Those are expected for a brand-new surface. Delaying #2 for them starves the experiment of a second data point.

### Why two videos are still too little for broad conclusions

One strong or weak film cannot prove or kill Deep Forest Dispatch. Topic cluster, packaging, and search need **multiple pieces and time**. Use #1 + #2 to compare packaging and retention patterns — not to declare the brand.

## Day-30 decision framework

Classify **evidence across the library + Video #1 (and #2 if live)** — not a single outlier.

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **KEEP** | Enough signals that DFD deserves continued production (some discovery, engagement, or clear packaging wins worth iterating). |
| **ADJUST** | Concept is viable but topic mix, titles/thumbs, openings, article search targeting, or distribution needs modification. |
| **PIVOT** | **Multiple** pieces over the period show little meaningful interest after time for distribution — reconsider approach. |

**Not a pivot:** one soft video, one article with no impressions yet, or a quiet first week.

## Known measurement limits

See also the checklist. Honest gaps:

1. **Waypoint DFD events** (`DFD_*`) emit to `waypoint:analytics` / `__WAYPOINT_ANALYTICS_QUEUE__`. There is **no built-in dashboard in-repo**. Totals are available only if the owner’s analytics consumer (e.g. GA4) records those events — otherwise capture via browser debug or treat as unavailable.
2. **YouTube → Waypoint** is not automatic. Visible only if GSC/analytics show referrers, YouTube description clicks show in YouTube analytics external/traffic where offered, or UTM links were used (DFD did not require UTMs for launch).
3. **GSC indexed count** is approximate (URL Inspection / pages reports lag).
4. **Average position** is noisy at tiny sample sizes — leave blank until impressions are meaningful.
5. **Subscribers gained “from this video”** depends on YouTube’s attribution UI; use the Studio figure and note uncertainty.

Do not build new analytics infrastructure for this scorecard.
