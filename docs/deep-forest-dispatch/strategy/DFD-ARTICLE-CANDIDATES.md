## Method notes

- Researched ~observable SERP composition and authoritative source availability (NASA, NOAA, USGS, parks, universities).
- **No invented search-volume numbers.** Scores are editorial prioritization judgments.
- Completed DFD stories are listed but **not** counted toward First 10.

## Score dimensions (1–5)

search · curiosity · Waypoint fit · visual · scientific depth · evergreen · original value · free-asset availability · internal-link potential · production effort *(higher = easier)*
