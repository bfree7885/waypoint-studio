## Strategic change

**Pause new DFD video production.** Do not begin Video #3.

For the next phase, Deep Forest Dispatch / Waypoint prioritizes a **high-quality article library**.

```text
WAYPOINT ARTICLE LIBRARY
+
SELECTIVE DFD VIDEOS FOR THE STRONGEST STORIES
```

not:

```text
EVERY ARTICLE REQUIRES A VIDEO
```

Videos remain valuable. Articles become the scalable discovery base. Future videos should be selected from articles that already prove curiosity, clarity, and engagement — not the reverse.

## Why now

Waypoint already has (or is shipping via PR #39):

- reusable DFD story infrastructure
- two completed DFD videos + companion stories
- SEO, sitemap, structured data
- provenance / source handling
- analytics hooks
- related-story functionality

The next objective is enough **excellent** content for search/discovery to produce real traffic data — without becoming an AI content farm.

## Editorial identity

Stories begin with:

**A REAL PLACE**  
+ **SOMETHING VISIBLE, STRANGE, BEAUTIFUL, COUNTERINTUITIVE, OR EASY TO MISREAD**  
+ **THE EARTH-SCIENCE / GEOGRAPHIC EXPLANATION**

Reader arc:

1. I saw something interesting.  
2. Why does that happen?  
3. Oh — now I understand what I’m looking at.

Aligned with Waypoint: **Observe → Discover → Understand → Explore**.

## Non-negotiables

- No mass thin SEO pages
- No keyword-variant article sprawl
- No “people search for it” as sole justification
- No keyword → AI draft → publish
- Every page needs original visual/data value beyond rewritten prose
- Scientific claims trace to authoritative sources where practical
- Preserve provenance on imagery and datasets

## Waypoint advantage test

A topic is eligible only if Waypoint can add at least one of:

- real satellite evidence / change through time
- a clearer map or annotated landform
- an original mechanism graphic
- outdoor observation or photography connection
- honest link into existing Waypoint tools
- authoritative multi-source synthesis that is clearer than SERP alternatives

If the only answer is search demand: **reject**.

## Article quality model

Strong pages usually combine several of:

real photography · satellite imagery · map · data · before/after · time series · original graphic · useful interactive · field observation note · related Waypoint tool · authoritative sources · related DFD story

Not every element every time. Always something more than 1,000 words of generated prose.
