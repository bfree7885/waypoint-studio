**Status:** Proposed — awaiting owner/editorial approval

**Rule:** These are NEW stories. Mount Hood + Lençóis are already complete and excluded.

## Selection method

Not a blind top-10-by-score sort. Built for:

- portfolio diversity
- Waypoint original-value strength
- free NASA/NOAA/USGS-class assets
- limited overlap with completed stories
- mix of Flagship / Core / Quick / Video-candidate

Hawaii wet/dry scored high but was **deferred** as too similar to the Mount Hood rain-shadow story.

## Strongest search opportunities (observable SERP, not volumes)

1. **Great Salt Lake two colors** — clear “why” intent; results split across news, NASA EO, and local explainers; room for one unified visual page.  
2. **Valley fog / morning fog in valleys** — frequent outdoor noticing query; NWS pages are authoritative but rarely combine satellite dendritic pattern + field noticing + Waypoint tools.  
3. **Lenticular / “UFO” clouds** — high curiosity; photo-heavy SERPs with thinner mechanism explainers for outdoor photographers.

## Strongest visual opportunities

1. **Namib racing dunes** — multi-year Landsat time series (NASA EO already proves the visual).  
2. **Columbia Glacier** — decades of Landsat World of Change imagery.  
3. **Great Salt Lake color divide** — causeway line + north/south color contrast from orbit.
