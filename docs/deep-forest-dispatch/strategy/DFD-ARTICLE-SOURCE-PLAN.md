## Asset acquisition rules

- Prefer public-domain NASA/USGS/NOAA.
- Keep credit strings on-page and in asset README.
- Label educational derivatives as educational.
- Do not scrape copyrighted travel photography without license.
- When only secondary sources exist for a claim, mark confidence and seek primary confirmation before publish.

## Source ledger (required before draft)
