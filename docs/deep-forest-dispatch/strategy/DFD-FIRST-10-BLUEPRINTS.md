## 1. Why the Great Salt Lake is two colors

**Slug:** `great-salt-lake-two-colors`  
**Location:** Great Salt Lake, Utah  
**Type:** Flagship · Video-candidate

### Search intent
- Primary: why is Great Salt Lake two colors / half red half blue  
- Cluster: Great Salt Lake causeway salinity; north arm pink; Dunaliella / halophiles  

### Why click
Orbit and shoreline photos look impossible — a hard color line across one lake.

### After reading
Reader understands the railroad causeway restricted exchange, salinity diverged, and different microbes dominate each arm.

### WAYPOINT ORIGINAL VALUE
Annotated NASA/USGS image with causeway, north/south salinity contrast, and a simple microbe-pigment key — plus a short “what changed after the breach” note without overclaiming.

3. **What you’re seeing** — Causeway line; north Gunnison Bay vs south Gilbert Bay.  
4. **Why** — Restricted flow → higher north salinity → red carotenoid microbes/algae vs greener south communities.  
5. **Show me** — NASA EO astronaut/satellite color contrast + USGS multi-decadal change context.  
6. **Noticing** — Shoreline salt crusts can tint; smell/ecology are part of the same story (restrained).  
7. **Explore** — USGS Utah Water Science Center; NASA EO; related Lake Eyre story; Dashboard conditions if visiting Utah; Scenes for high-contrast lake light.

### SEO
- Meta: Why Utah’s Great Salt Lake looks half pink and half blue — causeway, salinity, and the microbes that paint the water.

## Shared writing bans (all ten)

Avoid: “Imagine…”, “Did you know…”, “In today’s world…”, “Whether you’re…”, fake quotes, keyword stuffing, repetitive thesis restatement, inspirational closers, textbook throat-clearing intros.

Prefer: place first, evidence second, mechanism third, noticing last.
