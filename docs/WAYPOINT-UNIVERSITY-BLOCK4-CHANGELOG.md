## Product

- **Scholar** becomes the primary research environment (`#scholar`) with eight workspaces.
- **Research sessions** — start, resume, complete, or park; discoveries feed the learning timeline.
- **Field notes** — quick capture for outings, tastings, investigations, troubleshooting; place/conditions/geo hooks.
- **Project research hubs** — sessions, notes, questions, sources, experiments, field notes, ideas.
- **Source reliability** — personal authority / evidence / bias / recency / confidence + conflicting viewpoints.
- **Thinking tools foundation** — concept maps, argument maps, decision journals, hypotheses, experiment plans as extensible stubs.
- Home CTA and brand subtitle point to Scholar.
