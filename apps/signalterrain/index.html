One calm place to open each day: a cyber awareness brief from verified public sources — what changed, why it matters, who may be affected, and defensive next steps. Not a news feed, not a SIEM, not offensive security.

Who it helps

Defensive-minded professionals and serious amateurs who need trustworthy public-source context without hype, fear, or attack tooling.

Primary workflow

  1. Open the live brief — trust label, refresh time, and provider health are visible up front.
  2. Read what changed — plain-language priorities with sources and retrieval times.
  3. Drill into a detail — evidence, risk framing, and defensive recommendations.

Honesty rules

Live surfaces use public-source artifacts with explicit trust states. If the brief is older than 36 hours, the UI downgrades “Live” to “Stale” so age is never disguised. Samples stay labeled samples.

Privacy

Persona preferences stay in your browser. Precise locations and private notes are not required for the cyber brief. See the site privacy policy.
