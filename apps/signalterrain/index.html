Foundation

SignalTerrain

SignalTerrain helps people observe and understand invisible environments — radio spectrum and educational cyber awareness — with evidence, context, and calm summaries. Not a news feed, not a SIEM, not offensive security.

Open intelligence summary

Who it helps

People who want to understand what changed in the radio environment or the public cyber landscape, why it matters, who may be affected, and what defensive next steps are worth considering — without hype, fear, or attack tooling.

Two major areas

  • Radio & Spectrum Intelligence

    SDR, receivers, propagation, monitoring, and RF observations — literacy and private tools, not intercept suites.

  • Cyber Awareness

    Ransomware, zero-day and exploited vulnerabilities, major attacks, outages, advisories, defensive guidance, and changing threat priorities — educational and defensive only. Not scanning, penetration testing, offense, SOC, IDS, or IPS.

Privacy philosophy

Receivers, audio, notes, and precise locations stay on your device first. Samples are labeled samples — never presented as live intelligence.

Future direction

Deeper RF observation tools, richer defensive cyber topic coverage, and carefully bounded future phases that remain educational — never offensive.
