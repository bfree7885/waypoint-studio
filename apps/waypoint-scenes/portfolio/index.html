Portfolio Advisor

A calm assistant that works on a Scene — not a separate upload flow. It will help you choose a balanced set of photographs and explain why.

Foundation in progress. Selection is not available yet. This page describes the intended capabilities so the path is clear. No fake results or invented rankings are shown.

Upcoming capabilities

  • Best-of selection from a Scene
  • Duplicate and near-duplicate grouping
  • Balanced portfolios (variety of subject, light, distance)
  • Storytelling sequences
  • Portfolio reasoning — why a frame belongs (or doesn’t)
