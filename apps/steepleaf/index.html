Who it helps

Curious drinkers and learners who want an interconnected understanding of tea — origins, craft, flavor, brewing, history, and where sample vendors list leaves.

Key capabilities

  • Shared knowledge graph — teas, places, producers, cultivars, processing, sensory notes
  • Search & discovery lenses with reasons for every recommendation
  • Educational entity pages — overview, facts, timeline, relations, AI summary
  • Private on-device catalog & brew journal (separate from the public graph)

Privacy philosophy

Brew notes stay private. There are no follower counts, influencers, or engagement tricks. Vendor offers in the sample graph are educational — not checkout.
