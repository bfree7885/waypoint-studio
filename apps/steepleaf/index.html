Foundation

Steepleaf

A refined tea companion — catalog your leaves, journal your brews, and notice flavor without social ranking.

Who it helps

People who brew with attention: what you steep, how it tastes, and what you want to try next.

Key capabilities

  • Tea catalog — origin, type, and tasting words
  • Brew journal — leaf, water, time, temperature, notes
  • Private collections and shelves

Privacy philosophy

Your catalog and brew journal stay on this device. There is no marketplace and no public tasting board.
