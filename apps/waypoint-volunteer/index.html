Anyone looking for a calm way to contribute — without pressure, rankings, or gamification.

Examples of good work

  • Animal shelters and wildlife care
  • Conservation and habitat restoration
  • Trail work and park stewardship
  • Citizen science and educational programs
  • Community cleanup and habitat planting
  • Food banks, libraries, and local events

How recommendations will adapt

Suggestions already respect sample weather, season, distance, skills, time, family, and accessibility filters — still discovery only, still without streaks or leaderboards. Location uses your device when allowed; otherwise a labeled sample region.

Citizen science bridges

Bird surveys and habitat days point you to Fieldry, Sheds, ForageCast, Scenes, and Landscape Interpretation for continued observation — Volunteer stays the discovery door, not a second data model.

Privacy philosophy

No hours tracking. No public activity feed. Sample data is labeled. You contact organizations directly.
