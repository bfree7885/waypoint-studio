Field ethics & honesty

      
    
        
  • Property & law. This map does not grant legal access. Confirm permission and local shed seasons.
    • 
        
  • Wildlife. Do not chase, harass, or displace wintering deer. Approach bedding notes carefully.
    • 
        
  • Habitat. Avoid trampling sensitive areas; leave no trash; respect closures.
    • 
        
  • Privacy. Observations, tracks, and validations stay in this browser’s storage. Export creates a file you control.
    • 
        
  • Map providers. Tile requests (OSM / OpenTopoMap) and optional Open-Meteo elevation/weather reveal approximate location to those services.
    • 
        
  • Model honesty. The colored surface is relative search priority for whitetail — never “there is an antler here.”
    • 
        
  • Sensitive places. Do not publish precise private or protected locations from this tool.
