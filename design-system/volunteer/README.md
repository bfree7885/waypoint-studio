## Rules

1. Discovery only — not management or registration.  
2. No gamification (rankings, streaks, likes).  
3. Samples stay labeled `sample`.  
4. Citizen science bridges to Fieldry — not a separate social product.  
5. Today Outside hooks are documented; Dashboard widget remains honest until wired.
