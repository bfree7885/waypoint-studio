| Claim | Support |
| --- | --- |

## Caveats

- Do not claim plants create nutrients from nothing.
- Do not collapse to a single mechanism.
- Succession is nuanced; mid-dune protected sites often favor tall forest.
- Prefer parks/science framing over tourism copy.
