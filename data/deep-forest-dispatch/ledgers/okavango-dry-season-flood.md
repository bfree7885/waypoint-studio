# Source ledger — Why the Okavango Delta floods in the dry season

**Slug:** `okavango-dry-season-flood`  
**Date:** 2026-08-16

## Claim plan

| Claim | Support |
| --- | --- |
| Inland/alluvial fan delta in northern Botswana / Kalahari | NASA EO Okavango; UNESCO/context via EO |
| Flood primarily driven by seasonal rain in Angolan highlands (Cubango/Cuito catchments), not local Botswana rain | HESS Wolski & Murray-Hudson 2006; NASA EO Kalahari/Okavango |
| Shallow gradient + long travel → delayed flood wave | HESS 2006; NASA EO captions |
| Inlet flood often peaks ~April; max inundation often Aug/Sep (out of phase with Nov–Mar rains) | HESS 2006 Fig.2 discussion |
| Inundated area expands from thousands of km² minimum to larger seasonal maximum | HESS 2006 ranges |
| Most water lost to evapotranspiration / does not reach the sea (endorheic) | NASA EO ISS caption (~2% exits) |
| Year-to-year timing and extent vary | HESS; NASA early-flood 2012 note |

## Caveats

- Do not claim a single fixed “takes exactly N months” for every year.
- Local heavy rainfall can matter some years; average story is delayed upstream pulse.
- Diagram is schematic geography, not a surveyed hydrograph.
