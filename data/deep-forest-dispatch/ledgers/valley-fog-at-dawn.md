## Caveats

- Do not invent a Waypoint “fog forecast” product.
- Distinguish radiation/valley fog from advection fog, upslope fog, and steam fog.
- Multi-day winter valley fog / inversions (e.g. persistent cold pools) can last longer than classic dawn burn-off — mention lightly without expanding into a second essay.
- NASA Australian Alps example illustrates dendritic valley fill; mechanism is general, location is an example.
