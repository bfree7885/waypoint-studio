## Claim plan

| Claim | Support |
| --- | --- |
| Kati Thanda–Lake Eyre is usually a dry / salt-crust terminal basin | NASA EO 154451; Australian Lake Eyre Basin materials |
| ~1/6 of Australia drains toward the lake (endorheic) | NASA EO; DCCEEW Lake Eyre Basin |
| Floodwaters often travel hundreds of km; much evaporates / soaks before arrival | NASA EO Cooper Creek / Water Pours |
| Partial fills occur more often than complete fills | NASA EO: complete fills only a few times in ~160 years |
| Exceptional fills historically include ~1950, 1974, 1984; 1974 ~6 m depth | NASA EO |
| 2025 pulse was a major partial/near-major fill event observed by MODIS | NASA EO 154451, 154803, Lake Eyre Blushes |
| Wildlife response (brine shrimp, waterbirds) follows inundation | NASA EO narrative |

## Caveats

- Do not say the lake fills every year.
- “Inland sea” is figurative for a temporary large water body — not a marine sea.
- Distinguish partial flooding from rare complete fills.
- Evaporation dominates once inflows stop.
