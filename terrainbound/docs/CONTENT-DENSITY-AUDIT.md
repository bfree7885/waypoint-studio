# TerrainBound Content Density Audit

Reusable design infrastructure for every future region. Do not treat this as a worksheet for students.

**CONTENT-HEAVY UNDERNEATH. LIGHT-FEELING ON THE SURFACE.**

Scientific rigor lives in observation, measurement, comparison, modeling, prediction, explanation, revision, and transfer. Dialogue stays short.

## How to use

Complete one table per major concept or skill **before** building a region. Revisit it after playtest.

Learning progression for each skill:

NOTICE → MEASURE / COMPARE → USE → TRANSFER

Transfer means a later problem requires the student to recognize and apply prior knowledge without the game reteaching it.

## Region layers

Every region must contain:

| Layer | Purpose | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Required core | Genuine demonstration of required understanding | Cannot be skipped for mastery |
| B. Embedded practice | Short natural reuse during exploration | Does not feel like drill |
| C. Optional depth | Extra learning | Must not be required to open the next route |

Do not use C to compensate for a weak A.

## Cumulative lock

Regions are not isolated units.

| Origin | What must keep mattering |
| --- | --- |
| Cedar Hollow | Scientific reasoning, fair tests, evidence vs completion |
| High Country | Maps, coordinates, terrain reading, route decisions |
| Sunfall | Daily sky, seasons, orbits, Moon geometry where relevant |

Future regions should deliberately reuse earlier tools. Do not reteach by lecture.

## Play rhythm check

After a draft region, walk it and mark:

- stretches of 2–5 minutes of movement / world observation
- focused scientific interactions of about 1–3 minutes
- returns to walking

Flag any long uninterrupted tablet sequence that could have been a world action.
