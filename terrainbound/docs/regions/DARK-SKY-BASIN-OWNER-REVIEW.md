# Dark Sky Basin — Owner Review (Phase 8A)

**Design only. Do not implement 8B from this file until you say GO.**

## Pitch

Dark Sky Basin is a high desert hollow whose rims still keep the night dark enough to work. The Field Service inherited a log that treats two bright white stars as twins because they look the same in the eyepiece. Wren brings you here after Sunfall because the Sun is a star you can time; these are stars you will never visit. The job is to read what their light can honestly say — and the basin’s lamp bench, opposite rim cairns, and quiet floor are why that work can happen here at all.

## Curriculum (locked)

Topic **11 — Stars & the Universe**. Not a second Solar System region. Sunfall already owns seasons, Moon, orbits, and the celestial clock.

Leave with: spectra as evidence, temperature from color/peak, brightness ≠ distance, diagram from *your* measurements, mass-dependent lives, heavy elements from stars, redshift as a pattern, origin claims that need more than a label, lookback time, and the discipline to stop talking.

## What is actually fun

Standing in a place that is *dark on purpose*. Walking the rim because distance is a place, not a formula. A lamp you have to leave the dome to use. Two stars that looked like twins. A radio horn on the salt pan. Summit as Sasquatch who will not let fire-color folklore slide — and will not clear you.

## What might feel school-like

Spectrograph matching if it becomes multiple-choice element bingo. An HR diagram with a legend. Indoor plate drawers with no walk. Galaxy redshift as a graph worksheet. “Big Bang” as a vocabulary stamp. Constellation quizzes.

**Design rule:** if the explorer could do it without leaving the dome, it is not ready.

## Highest-risk mechanic

**Spectra-as-worksheet.** If DS-01 is a panel of questions, the region fails even if the science is correct.

Second risk: rebuilding Sunfall’s sky clock and teaching Moon/seasons again.

Third: hosted Summit inventing magnitudes.

## Recommended first playable slice (later 8B)

Night basin + station + lamp bench + two authored white stars. **DS-01**, then **DS-02**. Feel the dark, walk for a fair measurement, break one folklore. No galaxies, no horn, no clearance, no production deploy.
