OpenRoad PA

Tracking Pennsylvania's public road projects.

Follow construction projects, timelines, costs, and public records—and see how those records change over time.

In development

Early stage

OpenRoad PA is an early Waypoint project. There is no live application or dataset here yet. The aim is calm, evidence-first public information—what is under construction, where, reported costs and dates, and how those public records change.

Indicators will measure facts worth a closer look—never a guilt score, and never an accusation without evidence.
